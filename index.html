The Chef

Meet Jerome

Romey Rome

Born in Manhattan and raised between Bamberg, South Carolina and Norwich, Connecticut, Jerome Tucker — known to a 32,000-strong following as Romey Rome — built his palate at the elbow of his mother and aunts in a Southern kitchen.

The journey moved from home cook to public name in 2008. By 2015, the dinners had become events. Today, as a Bobby Brown Foods Culinary Ambassador and traveling celebrity chef out of the Bronx, Romey Rome serves Southern-inspired cuisine that reflects the full geography of his upbringing — New York energy, Carolina soul, Connecticut steadiness.
