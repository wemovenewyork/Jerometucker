The Chef

Meet Jerome

Romey Rome

A Bobby Brown Foods Culinary Ambassador with 32,000 followers and a decade of private dinners behind him, Jerome Tucker — known to most as Romey Rome — built his palate at the elbow of his mother and aunts in a Southern kitchen.

Born in Manhattan and raised between Bamberg, South Carolina and Norwich, Connecticut, his cooking reflects the full geography of that upbringing — New York energy, Carolina soul, Connecticut steadiness. What started as home cooking in 2008 became curated events by 2015, and today fuels a traveling celebrity chef practice booked out of the Bronx and across the country.
